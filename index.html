FAQ

Questions before you start?

Do I need to be an athlete to work with BioFlex?

No. BioFlex works with athletes, physique clients and everyday people who want expert training, better body composition, stronger habits or improved performance.

Can I work with you online?

Yes. Online and hybrid coaching are available depending on the service and your goal.

Do you work with schools, teams and organisations?

Yes. BioFlex can provide strength and conditioning, athlete testing, youth development, wellness talks, life-skills and stress-management workshops, and custom group programmes.

Is the wellness service therapy?

No. BioFlex wellness and psychosocial support is supportive and educational. It does not replace diagnosis, psychotherapy or treatment by an appropriately registered healthcare professional.

How do I know which service I need?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp us. Tell us your goal and situation and we will recommend the most suitable starting point.
